User Manual: Barista-Bot 3000 Coffee Robot.
Error code E-42 indicates that the system has run out of oat milk or the steam wand is clogged. 
To resolve error E-42, you must: 
1. Check the milk reservoir.
2. Rinse the steam wand with hot water at a pressure of 2 bars.
3. Press and hold the "Reset" button for 5 seconds.
If the red "Bean-Empty" indicator lights up, it means coffee beans are stuck in the grinder burrs. 
The use of caramelized beans is strictly prohibited, as this will void the warranty.
