The Nebula-9 mission was officially launched on April 14, 2029, from the Vostochny-2 cosmodrome. 
The primary goal of the expedition is to explore the exoplanet Gliese 581g, where traces of bioluminescent vegetation were previously detected. 
The crew consists of three members: Mission Commander Elena Markova, Flight Engineer Alexei Petrov, and Biologist Dr. Sarah Connor. 
The spacecraft is equipped with a Quasar-M antimatter engine, which allows it to reach the destination in 11 months. 
The project budget amounted to 45 billion credits.
