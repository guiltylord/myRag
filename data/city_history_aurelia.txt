The city of Aurelia was founded in 1452 by Captain Elara Reed on the shores of the Blue Bay. 
The city is famous for its "floating stones"—levitating rock fragments held in the air by magnetic anomalies. 
In 1678, the city experienced the "Great Drought," when the water level in the bay dropped by 15 meters. 
The main attraction is the Tower of Winds, built entirely of pure obsidian. 
The entrance fee for tourists is 10 gold coins, while local residents can visit for free on Thursdays.
